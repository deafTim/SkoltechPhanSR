\section{Problem Formulation}
\label{sec:problem}

Let \(f_{\theta_0}\) be a pretrained super-resolution (SR) model that maps a low-resolution image \(x_{\mathrm{LR}}\) to a high-resolution reconstruction
\begin{equation}
  x_{\mathrm{ref}} = f_{\theta_0}(x_{\mathrm{LR}}).
\end{equation}
The image \(x_{\mathrm{ref}}\) is the \emph{reference} throughout. The goal is not to improve reconstruction relative to ground-truth HR; it is to adapt the SR model so that its output becomes more compressible under a fixed downstream codec while staying faithful to \(x_{\mathrm{ref}}\).

Let \(\theta\) denote trainable parameters initialised from \(\theta_0\), and define
\begin{equation}
  x = f_{\theta}(x_{\mathrm{LR}}), \qquad
  y = C(x), \qquad
  \hat{x} = D(y),
  \label{eq:pipeline}
\end{equation}
where \(C\) and \(D\) are the encoder and decoder of a fixed neural image codec, \(y\) is the latent code, and \(\hat{x}\) is the decompressed reconstruction. The bitrate associated with \(y\) is estimated by the codec entropy model and denoted \(R(y)\).

Fidelity is measured by PSNR (pixel values in \([0,1]\)):
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{PSNR}(a,b)
  = 10\log_{10}\!\left(\frac{CHW}{\|a-b\|_2^2}\right).
\end{equation}

The constrained optimisation problem is
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
    \min_{\theta}\quad
    & R\!\bigl(C(f_{\theta}(x_{\mathrm{LR}}))\bigr) \\
    \text{s.t.}\quad
    & \mathrm{PSNR}\bigl(f_{\theta}(x_{\mathrm{LR}}),\, x_{\mathrm{ref}}\bigr)
      \ge P_{\mathrm{target}}, \\
    & \mathrm{PSNR}\bigl(D(C(f_{\theta}(x_{\mathrm{LR}}))),\, x_{\mathrm{ref}}\bigr)
      \ge P_{\mathrm{target}}.
  \end{aligned}
  \label{eq:compression_aware_problem}
\end{equation}

Design choices that define the setting:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item the codec stays fixed; only the SR model is adapted;
    \item no ground-truth HR as the optimisation reference---only \(x_{\mathrm{ref}}\);
    \item adaptation is \emph{per-image}, because compressibility depends strongly on image-specific statistics.
\end{enumerate}
